## /intro — first reply to a new message

Hi, this is Weiye at China Hospitals Guide.

We help international patients find and coordinate treatment at top public hospitals in China. We're independent — we don't take commissions from hospitals, so our advice isn't tied to any one of them.

Three things help me give you a real answer instead of a generic one:
1. Your condition or diagnosis — and whether it's confirmed
2. What you're after: a second opinion, a specific treatment, or full travel coordination
3. Your country and rough timeline

Send records here anytime, in your own language. First step is free and I'll tell you honestly whether China is worth pursuing for your case.

## /ask — case details

To give you a real answer rather than a brochure, I need:

1. Condition / diagnosis — confirmed or still suspected
2. Stage or severity, and current symptoms
3. What treatment has been done or recommended so far
4. Records you already have — CT / MRI / PET-CT / pathology / bloods
5. Where you're being treated now
6. Your country and city (visa and travel planning)
7. How soon you want to travel
8. What you want from us: a second opinion, whether a hospital will accept you, or full coordination

Reports in any language are fine. Send them here or to contact@chinahospitalsguide.com.

## /services — what we do and don't do

What we do:
• Match hospitals to your condition — top-tier public hospitals with international departments
• Prepare your case file and send it to hospitals in the format they require
• Bring back their answers in writing, in English — will they accept you, cost range, treatment duration, earliest available date
• Visa invitation letter, appointment booking, airport pickup
• A named coordinator while you're in China, and an English discharge summary afterwards

What we don't do:
• Promise outcomes — the doctors decide, not us
• Set or cap hospital fees; treatment costs are paid to the hospital, not to us
• Take commissions from hospitals

## /how — how it works

Step by step:

1. You tell me your case — a few messages here is enough to start
2. Free case review: we tell you whether China makes sense and which hospital fits
3. If you want a firm answer, we contact the hospitals and bring back their written reply in English
4. If you decide to travel, we prepare your file, obtain the visa invitation letter, book your appointment and arrange pickup
5. While you're in China, we're your channel to the hospital
6. After discharge, you get an English summary for your doctor at home

You decide at each step. Nothing happens automatically.

## /records — what to send

Send whatever you already have — don't wait until it's complete:

• Discharge summaries or doctor's letters
• CT / MRI / PET-CT reports (the written report matters most; images too if you have them)
• Pathology or biopsy report
• Blood test results
• Current medication list
• A few lines in your own words: what's happening and what you want to know

Send here in WhatsApp, or by email to contact@chinahospitalsguide.com. Your own language is fine — we translate.

## /timeline — how long things take

Typical timing:

• Free case review: 1–2 business days
• Hospital Acceptance Check: 4–7 business days after we receive your records
• MDT Review: 5–10 business days
• Visa invitation letter + appointment: 1–3 weeks, depending on the hospital and your dates
• First message to landing in China: for most cases, 6–10 weeks

If your case is urgent, tell me now and I'll flag it. I won't promise speed the hospital can't deliver — I'll tell you what's realistic.
