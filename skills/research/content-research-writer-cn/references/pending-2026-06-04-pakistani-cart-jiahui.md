## Story

A 36-year-old Pakistani hospital worker from Quetta named Muhammad, with Stage IVB diffuse large B-cell lymphoma (DLBCL) that had relapsed after multiple lines of chemotherapy and a failed bone marrow transplant, achieved encouraging early recovery after personalized CAR-T therapy at Jiahui International Cancer Center in Shanghai.

Key facts:
- Patient: Muhammad, 36, hospital support staff from Quetta, Pakistan
- Diagnosis: Diffuse large B-cell lymphoma (DLBCL), Stage IVB with lung/pleural/bone metastasis
- Prior treatment: Multiple chemotherapy lines, one failed bone marrow transplant, disease never went into remission
- Treatment: Personalized CAR-T (autologous) at Jiahui International Cancer Center, Shanghai
- Lead physician: Dr. Hao Siguo, Chief Hematology Consultant (CAR-T clinical research since 2017)
- Bridging therapy: Rituximab + polatuzumab vedotin + 5 days bridging radiotherapy to reduce tumor burden
- Lymphocyte collection: April 3, 2026
- Lymphodepletion: April 17-19, 2026 (3 days)
- CAR-T cell infusion: April 23, 2026
- Outcome: Mild manageable cytokine release syndrome, no severe neurotoxicity, significant pain relief, opioid reduction, shrinking lesions, normalized labs
- Discharge: May 7, 2026
- Follow-up: Remote imaging and monitoring from Pakistan
- International support: Professional translation, halal catering, medical visa assistance, hospital pre-arrival coordination
- Patient quote: "Chinese people are very cooperative. The doctors, nurses, and staff were very supportive."
- Why he chose Jiahui: Fast response, clear guidance, strong CAR-T experience in complex cases. Also evaluated Germany, Singapore, Austria, India, US, Australia.
