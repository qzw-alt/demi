# Reliable sources for AI industry daily briefs

Use these starting points and adapt as sources change.

## Trending / aggregator
- Hacker News front page: `https://news.ycombinator.com/`
- Hacker News search: `https://hn.algolia.com/?q=openai%20anthropic%20google%20gemini`

## Tech media
- The Verge AI section: `https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence`
- TechCrunch AI section: `https://techcrunch.com/category/artificial-intelligence/`
- TechCrunch Venture / Startups / Security for funding and security stories

## Company blogs (use jina.ai if blocked)
- OpenAI blog / news: `https://openai.com/index/` and `https://openai.com/news/`
- Anthropic news: `https://www.anthropic.com/news`
- Google DeepMind blog: `https://deepmind.google/blog/`
- Google AI blog: `https://blog.google/technology/ai/`

## Bypass bot detection
- For pages that return Cloudflare challenges in the browser, fetch via:
  `https://r.jina.ai/http://example.com/path/`
- Works well for OpenAI blog posts and many paywalled/JS-heavy articles.

## Policy / regulation
- TechCrunch Government & Policy: `https://techcrunch.com/category/government-policy/`
- The Verge Policy: `https://www.theverge.com/policy`

## Tip
Open multiple sources in parallel. Do not rely on a single aggregator headline; verify numbers in the article body or official post.
