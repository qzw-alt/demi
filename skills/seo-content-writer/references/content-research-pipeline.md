# Content Research & Writer Pipeline

> **Source**: `content-research-writer` skill (absorbed into `seo-content-writer`)

## Standard Workflow

```
1. multi-search → research topics/hot trends
2. programmatic-seo → structure and write article  
3. humanizer → remove AI patterns
```

## Step 1: Research with multi-search

Use DuckDuckGo search to find:
- Current news/trends on the topic
- Data points and statistics
- Different perspectives and angles
- Source material for quotes

Search queries should cover:
- Main topic keywords
- Related industry news (last 24-48 hours)
- Statistics and data
- Expert opinions

## Step 2: Write with programmatic-seo

Structure article using:
- SEO best practices (title, headings, meta)
- News article Schema (NewsArticle)
- Clear narrative flow
- Data-backed claims with sources

## Step 3: Humanize with humanizer

Apply humanizer skill to remove:
- AI vocabulary (delve, tapestry, pivotal, etc.)
- Formulaic phrases
- Excessive hedging
- Promotional tone
- Chatbot artifacts

## Skills Used

- `duckduckgo-search` - for research
- `humanizer` - for de-AI writing
- `programmatic-seo` - (TBD - needs to be installed)

## Output

Final article should:
- Sound natural and human-written
- Have clear structure
- Include proper Schema markup
- Be ready for publication
