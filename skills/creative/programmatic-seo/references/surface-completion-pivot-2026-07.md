# Surface-Completion Pivot (verified 2026-07-27, oriental-destiny.com)

The companion pattern to the outdoor-to-indoor pivot. After a closed indoor thread (5+ indoor rooms in sequence), pivot back OUTDOOR with the SURFACE the 5-gate outdoor walk read across — the garden path. Walking the path closes the surface loop the gates opened.

## Why this pattern exists

The July 2026 outdoor 5-gate walk (Stairway 07-17 → Balcony 07-18 → Courtyard 07-19 → Side Yard 07-21) shared one feature that all five articles assumed without measuring: the path under the dragon's feet between gates. The indoor thread that followed (Living 07-22 → Dining 07-23 → Kitchen 07-24 → Bedroom 07-25 → Study 07-26) closed on 07-26 with explicit forward-anchor language ("The week to come will fold what these rooms say into a smaller, single-room read"). The 07-27 article walked the surface, satisfying BOTH the closed indoor anchor AND the closed outdoor anchor in a single lead paragraph.

## Bridge-sentence recipe (verified 07-27)

- **Lead paragraph** explicitly reads both the closed indoor thread AND the closed outdoor 5-gate walk: "Yesterday's study read closed the indoor thread. The week before that, the side yard read closed the outdoor five-gate walk. Today's piece walks the surface those five gates share."
- **Body section** names each of the 5 gates in order with the dragon's pace at each, then says "None of them reads the surface under the dragon's feet."
- **Closing section** ("How the X read ties back to the July thread"): "The July thread has now walked N outdoor reads. [list the 5 gates + the surface]. The N reads together form the full outdoor property read the homeowner needs before the Fire-to-Earth handoff completes later this month."
- **Forward-anchor paragraph** ("What to do this week" section): "The week to come will fold what [surface] says into a smaller, single-property read" — sets up the next BaZi-overlay article that takes the now-complete outdoor read and applies it to a specific chart.

## When to use this pattern

- The outdoor 5-gate walk is complete (5+ outdoor gate articles shipped in sequence)
- The indoor thread has also just closed (5+ indoor rooms in sequence)
- A surface-level outdoor feature exists that all the gates assumed but none measured
- The forward-anchor of the closed indoor thread explicitly invites the next article

## When NOT to use this pattern

- The outdoor 5-gate walk is still in progress (mid-thread; do not pivot to surface mid-walk)
- The indoor thread didn't close cleanly (no forward-anchor paragraph)
- A more obvious referenced-but-never-covered term exists (use that simpler pivot first)
- The surface has been given its own article already (de-dup grep `grep -lE "(garden path|patio|garden wall)" fate-2026-0[5-7]*.html` should return 0)
