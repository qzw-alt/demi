## Pivot Day Pattern (NEW, verified 2026-07-07)

### What it is
The day a solar-term handoff lands (Li Qiu, Xia Zhi, winter solstice, etc.) is the **pivot day** between two threads. The article written on the pivot day is the bridge — it opens the next thread by reading the room that connects to the prior thread, in the new season's frame.

### Verified pivot-day execution recipe

1. **The day before the pivot day** is the close-out / Eve piece (07-05 = outdoor close-out, 07-06 = Li Qiu Eve). That article should explicitly preview the next thread. The 07-06 article ended with: *"The indoor rooms have their own close-out coming later in July, and the indoor eve read is part of that piece, not this one."* — this is the cue for the pivot day.

2. **The pivot day itself** opens the new thread by writing the **channel-room** article — the room that connects every other room and reads the seasonal shift first. For indoor pivot days, the channel room is the **hallway**. For outdoor pivot days, the channel room is the **front garden** (Ming Tang).

3. **Why the channel room is the pivot-day lead piece**:
   - It's the only room that reads the seasonal shift in real time (the air flows through it; light changes hit it first)
   - It has never been covered as a standalone pillar piece (40+ articles on the site had no hallway article before 07-07)
   - It frames the entire next thread: each subsequent article reads a room FROM the channel room, so the reader who lands on the pivot-day piece gets the routing map for the thread
   - Its seasonal element matches the new season (Earth Month = channels/settling, so the hallway — a channel — is the right room to lead)

4. **The pivot-day article structure** (verified 07-07 hallway, 4,889 bytes / ~3,610 words):
   - Lead paragraph: thread-continuity bridge ("The June thread walked five indoor rooms... The July thread walked four outdoor rooms... The first indoor-room read of the new thread is the hallway, because...")
   - Pullquote: "The hallway is the room that does not have its own purpose, and that is its purpose."
   - Section 1: Why this room gets the pivot-day article
   - Section 2: What the room is asking for in the new season (3 things, in priority order)
   - Section 3: Door-by-door read for the rooms that connect to it
   - Section 4: Per-chart (Day Master) breakdown
   - Section 5: Seven-day pass timeline (the working read for the first week of the new season)
   - FAQ: 7 questions (narrow hallways, stairways, no-windows hallways, storage closets, rentals, thread-connection, open-plan)
   - Footer cross-links: prior thread's last 3 articles + 2 evergreen resources (Five Elements, Bagua Map)

5. **The 3 things the hallway asked for in Earth Month** (template for future pivot-day channel articles):
   - Air (open doors at both ends; 6-inch gaps)
   - Light (warm-white bulbs at 60% brightness on a dimmer; cool-white is the wrong-season read)
   - Floor (runner rug, two-thirds corridor length, warm tone)

   For other pivot-day channel rooms, the 3 priorities shift: front garden = path, gate, Earth anchor. Living room (when it pivots) = sofa, coffee table, lighting. Substitute as appropriate.

```
Is today a solar-term landing day? (Li Qiu, Xia Zhi, Li Dong, Dong Zhi, Chun Fen, etc.)
  YES → PIVOT DAY. Write the channel-room article for the NEW thread.
  NO ↓

Are we mid-thread? (i.e., last 1-3 articles were on the same sub-topic like outdoor rooms)
  YES → Continue the room walk. If 4+ articles done in this sub-topic, write the CLOSE-OUT (07-05 pattern). If close-out already done, write the EVE PIECE (07-06 pattern) for the upcoming solar term.
  NO ↓

Is the prior article's last paragraph previewing a future thread?
  YES → Open that thread today with the channel-room article (pivot day logic applies even when not on the solar-term day itself)
  NO ↓

Use the standard thread continuity bridge:
  - If last 5+ articles were indoor rooms, the next article should pivot to outdoor or to a foundational concept
  - If last 5+ articles were on one element, the next should be on the next element in the seasonal cycle
  - If last 5+ articles were English-language term primers, the next should be a Chinese-term primer (or vice versa)
  - Always mine prior articles for "referenced-but-never-covered" terms (the 06-19 / 06-26 pattern)
```

The stairway reads the vertical dimension; the balcony reads the in-between horizontal dimension. ### The "vertical-gate → in-between-gate" pivot (NEW, verified 2026-07-18)

This is a NEW pivot pattern distinct from the 07-07 channel-room pivot. When the prior article is a **vertical-gate read** (stairway, elevator shaft, light well), the next article should be the **in-between gate read** — the space that catches the dragon between the interior and the exterior. For stairway → balcony, the read extends naturally because the stairway's Day Master overlay (top landing for Wood/Fire, bottom landing for descending/Earth) maps cleanly to the balcony's Day Master overlay (lean-into balcony position per element).

**Verified pivot-day execution recipe (extended 07-18):**

1. **Identify what gate the prior article read.** Vertical gate (stairway, light well)? Horizontal gate (the three gates from 07-11)? Chart overlay (Day Master, chart branch)? Outdoor read (front garden, back garden)?
2. **Pick the read that completes the property scan but isn't redundant:**
   - After vertical gate → in-between gate (balcony, terrace, sunroom)
   - After horizontal gate → vertical gate (stairway)
   - After chart overlay → vertical gate or property-type variant
   - After outdoor room → indoor room (channel-room pivot) or another outdoor room in the same size class
3. **Bridge sentence pattern (verified 07-18):** the lead paragraph of the new article should name the prior article explicitly ("The stairway read from yesterday walked the vertical gate..."), then name what the new article reads that the prior article missed ("today's piece walks the gate most homeowners neither see nor name"). This bridges the thread without repeating the prior article's content.
4. **Day Master overlay extension:** the new article should extend the Day Master overlay from the prior article to the new gate in a 1-paragraph section. This is what kept the 07-18 article's humanize score at 95 on first pass — the overlay extension is the bridge from prior-thread to current-thread without restating prior content.
