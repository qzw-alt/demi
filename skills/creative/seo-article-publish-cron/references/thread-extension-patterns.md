# Thread Extension Patterns (verified 2026-06 through 2026-07)

Companion to the `seo-article-publish-cron` skill's "Topic threading" section. Three verified patterns for extending a content thread across multiple days without breaking continuity or burning the topic prematurely.

## 1. Scaled-companion extension (NEW — verified 2026-07-03)

**When to use:** the current thread assumes a property the majority of readers don't have. Write a companion piece that covers the same concept at a different scale.

**Verified case (2026-07-03 oriental-destiny.com Balcony):**
- Day 1 (07-01): Front Garden Feng Shui for July (Ming Tang — the seasonal qi entry)
- Day 2 (07-02): Back Garden Feng Shui for July (Fu Wei — the homeowner's work-yard)
- Day 3 (07-03): Balcony Feng Shui for July (small Fu Wei — apartment-dweller counterpart)

The 07-02 article assumed the reader has a back garden. The 07-03 article extended the series by recognizing that most readers have a balcony instead, not a garden. The 07-03 lead explicitly says: "Most homeowner-level feng shui articles assume the reader has a garden. The two prior pieces this week assumed the reader has a gate, a bright hall, a back fence. That assumption is wrong for a large share of readers."

**Recipe:**
1. Confirm the previous day's concept reads at the new scale (Fu Wei = back garden, small Fu Wei = balcony; Ming Tang = entry hall, small Ming Tang = front door mat)
2. Keep the SAME structural skeleton so the series reads coherent (bridge sentence → 5 ordered moves → section-by-section read → Day-Master-by-element variation → undo at Li Qiu → FAQs)
3. Swap the property-specific moves for the smaller-scale moves
4. Add 1-2 FAQs for the scale-specific edge cases (Juliet rail with no standing room, shared balcony with a dividing screen, sun room, close-facing wall, concrete floor)
5. Use the same close-out (the next solar-term checkpoint) so the series lands on a shared checkpoint

**Why this works for SEO:** Google ranks topical clusters. A site with "Front Garden" + "Back Garden" + "Balcony" reads as a complete outdoor-feng-shui cluster; a site with only "Front + Back" reads as half-finished. The reader who lands on any one piece gets a complete answer for their property type.

**Why this works for the series voice:** the bridge sentence explicitly references yesterday's piece, so the reader who lands on Day 3 first can navigate back to Day 1 and 2 via the footer cross-links. The "scaled-companion" framing also signals to the reader that the site is paying attention to the actual housing stock of the audience (most US/UK readers are apartment dwellers, not homeowners with full gardens).

**Generalizes to:**
- "House" → "Apartment" (any property-specific piece)
- "Full property tour" → "Single room tour" (depth vs breadth)
- "Adult chart" → "Child chart" (audience scope)
- "US healthcare pathway" → "Cross-border healthcare pathway" (geography)
- "Surgery" → "Outpatient procedure" (procedure intensity)

The pattern: when a previous piece assumed X, write the companion that covers the readers who don't have X. Same concept, scaled to the reader's reality.

## Pitfalls

**Don't use the scaled-companion pattern on a thread that doesn't have a property assumption.** The pattern works because Day 2 made a property assumption. If Day 2 was a concept explainer (e.g. "What is a Sheng cycle?"), there's no property scale to down-scale to. Use the referenced-but-never-covered pivot instead.

**Don't extend a thread past its natural end.** The scaled-companion pattern extends a thread by ONE piece (the apartment-reader companion), not by a full 5-day series. After the companion, pivot to a different sub-thread (the referenced-but-never-covered pivot or a new concept). The reader will start to notice if "outdoor feng shui for X-property-type" runs for 5 days in a row.

**Don't forget the shared close-out.** If the original thread was building toward a specific checkpoint (the July 7 Li Qiu, the August mid-month, the autumn equinox), the scaled-companion piece must use the SAME close-out date. The 07-02 and 07-03 articles both use "undo at Li Qiu on July 7" as their close-out, which makes the series land on a shared seasonal moment.
