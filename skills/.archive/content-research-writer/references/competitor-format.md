# Competitor Format Reference — MyChinaMed.com

> Source: Real-time analysis, 2026-05-06
> Use when refreshing existing articles or writing new ones to match competitor quality

## Title Formula
```
[Procedure] in China: Cost, Top [Specialty] Hospitals & What Foreign Patients Need to Know
```
Examples:
- "Rhinoplasty in China: Cost, Top Hospitals & What Foreign Patients Need to Know"
- "CAR-T Therapy in China: Cost, Top Oncology Hospitals & What Foreign Patients Need to Know"

## Fixed Content Elements (every competitor article has all of these)
1. **Cost in USD** — specific ranges at named hospitals, not vague percentages
2. **Country comparison table** — vs US (sometimes UK/Australia) with named hospitals
3. **Hospital recommendations** — 3-5 named hospitals with specific doctors, credentials
4. **"Foreign patients" angle** — visa info, language support, how to prepare
5. **Complete guide feel** — FAQ section, step-by-step process, risks

## Quality Checklist (per new article standard)
- [ ] Cost: specific USD ranges at named hospitals (not "30-50% cheaper")
- [ ] Comparison: named US hospitals with specific prices
- [ ] Patient case: 1+ anonymized case with actual cost paid
- [ ] Expert: named + institution + specific data + source
- [ ] Policy: NMPA approval dates and current approved product count
- [ ] Risks: 2+ specific contraindications listed honestly
- [ ] Hospitals: named with specific details
