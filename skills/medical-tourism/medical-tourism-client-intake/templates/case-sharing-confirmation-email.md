We will work with whatever you feel comfortable sharing and will always send anything you produce to you for approval before it is published.

## 用法要点

- 一定要写明 "airport pickup is not included" — 模糊化会引发纠纷
- 时限用 "2-3 business days" — 伟烨在 Maria 案定的标准 SLA
- 案例分享用 "no deadline, no script, no pressure to be polished" — 降低患者心理门槛
- 强调 "for approval before it is published" — 隐私保护承诺
- 签名用 Weiye（个人），不是 Team — 增加温度（伟烨 2026-07-07 决定）
