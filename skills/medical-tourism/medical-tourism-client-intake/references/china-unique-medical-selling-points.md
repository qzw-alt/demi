These are procedurally unique — the hospital's institution literally pioneered them:

- **World's first** fully autonomous robotic implant procedure

- **Sell**: "The hospital that performed the world's first robot-led surgery"
- **Patient match**: Dental restoration, edentulism, complex implant cases

## Tier 2 — China's Global-Leading Volumes & Expertise

### 3. Microsurgery / Replantation (显微外科/断指再植)
- World's 1st toe-to-thumb transplant (Shanghai, 1966)
- China's military hospitals have **highest volume globally** for digit/limb replantation
- **Sell**: "Unmatched surgical volume = higher success rates" — Chinese surgeons see more of these cases than anyone else
- **Patient match**: Traumatic amputation (fingers, hands, limbs), nerve repair

### 4. Liver / Hepatobiliary Surgery (肝脏外科)
- Wu Mengchao's "five-lobe four-segment" theory (1960) is **global standard** for liver anatomy
- World's largest hepatic hemangioma removal (18kg, 1975)
- **Sell**: "The school of liver surgery that every liver surgeon in the world learns from"
- **Patient match**: Liver cancer (HCC), hepatic hemangioma, complex biliary disease

### 5. Organ Transplantation (器官移植)
- One of world's 3 largest transplant programs (10,000+/year)
- **Shortest wait times**: days vs. years in US/Europe
- **Cost**: Kidney ~$70K (vs. $300K+ US), Liver ~$160K (vs. $500K+ US)
- **Sell**: "Available when Western waitlists are too long or organs unavailable"
- **Patient match**: End-stage renal/liver disease, no donor in home country
