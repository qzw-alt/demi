## Overview

## 5. Content Structure Patterns

### Article Structure (Western SEO style):
1. **Title tag**: "Feng Shui [Topic] Guide: [Benefit]" or "[Number] Feng Shui [Topic] Tips"
2. **Meta description**: 150-160 characters with keyword
3. **H1**: Main keyword phrase
4. **H2 subheadings**: Numbered lists (e.g., "5 Feng Shui Tips for Your Bedroom")
5. **Introduction**: 100-150 words, keyword in first 50 words
6. **Body**: 800-1500 words for comprehensive guides
7. **FAQ section**: Schema markup, 5-8 questions
8. **Conclusion**: Call-to-action

### Common Content Length:
- **List articles**: 800-1200 words (5-10 items)
- **Ultimate guides**: 1500-3000 words
- **How-to tutorials**: 1000-1500 words
- **Room-specific**: 1200-1800 words

## 7. Vocabulary Notes for English Content

### Preferred spellings (Western standard):
- **Feng shui** (lowercase in running text, capital in titles)
- **Qi** (not "Chi" as primary - Qi is now dominant in Western usage)
- **Taoist** (not "Daoist" - more common in Western publications)
- **Yin and yang** (two words, lowercase)
- **Bagua** (not "Pa Kua")
- **I Ching** (capitalized, not "Yijing" in popular usage)

### Avoid:
- Direct word-for-word translations ("wind water" for feng shui)
- Oversimplified "energy" for qi (too vague)
- Wade-Giles romanization (use Hanyu Pinyin)
